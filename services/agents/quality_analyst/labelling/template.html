Call labelling sheet

How to fill this in (read once)
  1. Work alone. Do not look at the quality analyst page and do not compare with a teammate until you both have sent your file.
  2. For each call, read the conversation, then tick every problem that happened in it. If nothing went wrong, tick nothing.
  3. Press Save and next on every call, even when you tick nothing: that is how a call counts as done. Your work is saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back later (same browser, same file).
  4. When all calls are done, press Download my labels and send the file to Ghassen.
  5. Use the mood numbers (0 to 100, shown on customer lines when known). When there is none, judge the mood from the words. A mood under about 40 is upset.
  6. Check what the advisor says against the company facts below. If you are unsure, tick nothing and write a short note.
